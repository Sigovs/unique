About Unique Motors

We do not sell every car.
We find the ones worth keeping.

Unique Motors is a private sourcing partner and curated dealership for collectors, enthusiasts, and quiet connoisseurs. Each vehicle is chosen for its history, its honesty, and the way it drives — not its margin.

We work slowly, deliberately, and with a small client list. Most of what passes through the showroom never makes it to a public listing.
